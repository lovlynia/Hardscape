About Phoenix Stoneworks

Phoenix Stoneworks LLC, founded by Stephanie Abundio, has been building quality stone patios, walkways, and masonry throughout Connecticut for more than a decade — gaining her experience from her family.

Phoenix Stoneworks takes pride in delivering durable craftsmanship and attention to detail on every project, working closely with homeowners to create stonework that enhances both the beauty and value of their property.
